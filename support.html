Support

Questions, bug reports or feedback about Beyond the Nest? Leave us a message on our support page and we will get back to you.

github.com/mertdemirkale02-cpu/beyond-the-nest/issues

Please include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

How do I steer?

By default, hold the left or right edge of the screen to steer and tap the middle to jump. Tap again in the air for a Ground Pound. You can switch to tilt or joystick controls in Settings.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Beyond the Nest works fully offline. An internet connection is only used to load optional ads.

Why is the "+1 Life" button grey?

The extra life is given for watching a short ad. When no ad is available or you are offline, the button is disabled and you can continue with "Continue".

Where is my progress saved?

Your scores, coins, levels and settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. You can also reset progress in Settings.

Can I play in Turkish?

Yes. Open Settings and choose Türkçe.

Does the game track me?

No. See our Privacy Policy.
